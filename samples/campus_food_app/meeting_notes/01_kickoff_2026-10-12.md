# CampusBite kickoff - 12 Oct 2026

**Attendees:** Riya, Kabir, Meera, Dev, Prof. Iyer (first 15 minutes)

## Goal
An order-ahead app for the main canteen so students can skip the lunch queue.

## Scope for v1
- Browse menu, place an order, pay online, get a pick-up token
- Canteen staff screen to see and mark orders
- Out of scope: delivery to hostels, loyalty points

## Roles
- Riya: backend, database and payments
- Kabir: student-facing React app
- Meera: UI design, canteen staff interviews, demo script
- Dev: testing, test data, and deployment

## Timeline
- Working prototype by 14 Nov
- Final demo to the department panel on **10 Dec**

## Decisions
- Use Firebase for login and live order updates, to move fast.
- Take payments through the PayNest sandbox. Riya will apply for sandbox keys.
